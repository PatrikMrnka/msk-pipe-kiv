# Changelog

## [Unreleased]

### Added
- Project skeleton: pixi environments (`default`, `cpu`, `gpu`, `dev-cpu`), `mskpipe` CLI, CI on Windows.
- Step `mesh`: label map -> bone `.stl` / muscle `.obj` meshes in world coordinates (NIfTI RAS+, mm), Python VTK port of the BP `mask_to_mesh` (identical meshes with VTK 9.4.2), `meshes.json` index and per-structure metrics.
- `mskpipe.io` (NIfTI label maps, `labels.json` label table, STL/OBJ), `mskpipe.geometry` (surface extraction, mesh metrics, surface distances).
- Config `mesh.{bones,muscles}.min_component_fraction`.
- Step `labelmap`: raw tool segmentations (`01_segment/segmentation.json`) -> one cleaned uint8 label map (`labelmap.nii.gz`, `labels.json`, `labelmap.json` with per-structure metrics). Tool labels are mapped by name through `config/unified_labels.yaml`; TotalSegmentator `appendicular_bones` tibia/fibula are split by side; only the `skeleton.side` leg and the pelvis are built.
- Mask cleaning in mm (closing -> 3D hole filling -> opening -> components), growth only into background, one label per voxel (bones win over muscles). Validation against BP: `tools/labelmap_compare.py`, reference test `tests/steps/test_labelmap_reference.py`.
- Step `segment`: TotalSegmentator (`total`/`total_mr` by modality, `--roi_subset`, optional `appendicular_bones[_mr]`) and MuscleMap whole-body model (pinned 1.4) write raw multi-label images on the input grid plus `segmentation.json`; label maps are read from the tools (TotalSegmentator NIfTI extension, MuscleMap model JSON), never hard-coded. Both legs are segmented, so the step is reused for either side. Metrics: input grid, per-tool time and device, voxels per requested label, missing/empty labels.
- Cancellation of running tools (`ctx.cancel`): the tool and all its child processes are terminated, the step and run are recorded as `interrupted`.
- Skeleton: the STAPLE tibia body geometry is tibia + fibula of the same leg (BP convention, `skeleton.include_fibula`, merged mesh in `04_skeleton/bodies/`).
- `tools/skeleton_compare.py` (`mskpipe.validation.skeleton.compare_anatomical`): compare models built from different bones in the pelvis ACS (joint centres in mm, frame rotations in deg, markers, QC); `skeleton_parity.py` stays the same-bones parity check.
- `tools/segment_compare.py` (`mskpipe.validation.segment`): reference-free checks of the raw segmentations (all configured structures present, right of left in RAS, pelvis > femur > tibia superiorly) and comparison with reference masks (Dice, volume ratio, centroid shift, ASSD, HD95).
- Config `segmentation.musclemap.overlap` (90 as BP), `segmentation.musclemap.chunk_size`.
- Step `attachments`, plugin `bone_registration`: atlas bones registered onto the subject's bones (symmetric trimmed similarity ICP, point-to-point start + point-to-plane refinement) followed by a selectable non-rigid stage `nonrigid`: `coherent_icp` (default; non-rigid ICP with Gaussian motion-coherence regularisation and point-to-plane correspondences), `cpd` (Coherent Point Drift, Myronenko & Song 2010), `fast_rnrr` (Fast-RNRR, Yao et al. 2020; external executable supplied by the user, not distributed) or `none`. Attachment outlines transferred with their point order, snapped onto the bone and written in the Muscle Wrapping 2.x VTK layout; `attachments.json` index with per-bone registration residuals. Atlas via `attachments.params.atlas_dir` or `MSKPIPE_ATLAS_DIR` (not distributed).
- Sacrum as a separate bone (`sacrum`, label 8 = TotalSegmentator `sacrum` + `vertebrae_S1` in `total`; `total_mr` has no S1 label, labels a task lacks are left out of `--roi_subset`): segmented, cleaned and meshed like the other bones, not used by STAPLE (`pelvis_no_sacrum` only); step `attachments` registers `pelvis_with_sacrum` (hip bones + sacrum) so sacral attachment areas (gluteus maximus, piriformis) land on bone.
- `tools/attachments_compare.py` (run vs. reference outlines), `tools/slicer_view.py` (show a run in 3D Slicer).
- Step `export_mw2`: input folder for Muscle Wrapping 2.x (`06_mw2_input/`: `setup_MuscleGeneratorTool.xml`, model copy, `.mot`, `Geometry/<body>.obj`, `muscles/<m>/<Stem>.obj|_Ori.vtk|_Ins.vtk`, `export.json`). Files in mm with `scale_factors 0.001`, world RAS frame. Rest pose = pose of the image (all bodies on the ground frame): coordinate defaults and first motion row; joints that cannot reach it (STAPLE 1-DOF knee) get their child frame rotated (`export.align_child_frames`), the residual is reported. Motion: swept coordinate (default `hip_flexion_<s>`) from the rest pose to `export.motion.end_deg`. Pelvis geometry includes the sacrum (`export.pelvis_with_sacrum`), tibia includes the fibula.
- Pre-flight checks of every muscle as Muscle Wrapping needs it: closed 2-manifold, one component, genus 0, no degenerate triangles; attachment outlines are measured after projection onto the muscle (collapse, crossing, overlap, gap) and reported as warnings only (`outline_warnings`), they never exclude a muscle. Muscles excluded by default (`export.exclude`): piriformis, obturator internus, obturator externus and tensor fasciae latae; their insertion is reached through an unsegmented tendon or fascia running around a bony pulley or down to the tibia, so it cannot be placed on the segmented belly (reason per muscle in `export.json`, `excluded_by_config`). Repair (`export.repair`): mesh-level fixes, then remeshing of the mask with topology-preserving decimation: openings 1-3 mm (`export.repair_max_opening_mm`; cut thin bridges such as a muscle sheet left next to a bone after bone subtraction, the cause of genus 2 of the LHDL iliacus), closings (`export.repair_max_closing_mm`; fill tunnels), then both; a candidate is accepted only if `check_surface` passes and the volume changes by at most `export.repair_max_volume_change` (5 %); otherwise the best attempt is reported and the muscle excluded. Failing muscles are excluded or kept (`export.on_invalid`). Metrics: exclusions and reasons, repairs (change in mm), joint residuals, rest-pose displacement, tendon gap per outline, fibre ends nearer to another bone than their attachment body (Luca2018).
- `mskpipe.geometry.kinematics` (forward kinematics of `CustomJoint` models, image pose; checked against OpenSim 4.6), `mskpipe.geometry.topology`, `mskpipe.geometry.outline`, `mskpipe.io.mw2`; `.osim` reader also reads `SpatialTransform` axes and coordinate defaults; `mask_to_surface(decimation="topology")`.
- `mskpipe.geometry.frames`, `mskpipe.geometry.attachment_guard`; config `export.inflate_radius_mm` (5.0). Step `export_mw2` replaces an attachment outline whose projection onto the muscle is degenerate (fewer than 3 distinct points or a span below two mean edge lengths; Muscle Wrapping would cut an empty patch and crash) by the outline of a small patch of the muscle surface (`areas.<kind>.inflated` in `export.json`, warning `<muscle> <origin|insertion>: area inflated`); the muscle is kept. Metrics `inflated`, `times_s`, `coordinates_space`.
- `mskpipe run IMAGE -m ct|mri [-s] [-c] [--set k=v] [-d cpu|gpu|auto] [-o RUNS] [--no-cache] [--from/--until STEP] [--resume RUN] [--json]`: the whole pipeline up to the Muscle Wrapping 2.x input; exit codes 0 completed, 1 step failed, 2 invalid request/config/device, 130 cancelled. Replaces `tools/run_steps.py`.
- `mskpipe config init [PATH] | show [-c] [--set] [--json] | schema [-o]`.
- `mskpipe.api`: interface shared by the CLI, batch runs and the GUI (`RunRequest`, `prepare_run`, `preflight`, `run`, `RunEvent`, `RunSummary`, `summarize`, `finalize_stale`); runner callback `on_start`.
- Pre-run checks (`api.preflight`, plugin hook `Plugin.preflight`): NIfTI header (3D, qform/sform present, suspicious 1 x 1 x 1 mm spacing), plugin dependencies, TotalSegmentator licence key for `appendicular_bones`, attachment atlas and Fast-RNRR executable; errors stop `mskpipe run` before the segmentation (`--no-preflight` to override).

### Changed
- Step `export_mw2` writes its folder in the OpenSim frame (Y up): the skeleton model, bone and muscle meshes and attachment outlines are rotated by `RAS_TO_OPENSIM` (X anterior, Y superior, Z right; `coordinates.space = "opensim_yup"`, `coordinates.rotation_from_ras` in `export.json`); rest pose, child-frame alignment and outline checks run on the rotated data. Steps 01-05 stay in RAS. Attachment `.vtk` files are rewritten (not copied). Step version 3: earlier exports are not reused.
- Config `export`: `units` removed (Muscle Wrapping needs mm files with `scale_factors 0.001`); new `num_of_lines`, `line_res`, `decomposition_method`, `bone_weights`, `pelvis_with_sacrum`, `align_child_frames`, `motion`, `muscle_analysis`, `fibre_export`, `repair`, `repair_max_closing_mm`, `on_invalid`.
- Step fingerprints include the input modality (segmentation depends on it); caches of earlier runs are not reused.
- `biceps_femoris_*` = MuscleMap `biceps_femoris_long_head_*` (as BP).
- Default `segmentation.tibia_fibula = ts_appendicular`: tibia and fibula from TotalSegmentator `appendicular_bones` (as BP; needs a free academic licence key, `totalseg_set_license`); `musclemap` = no key.
- Config `labelmap.*`: `closing_radius_mm`, `opening_radius_mm` (default 1.5 mm = BP SimpleITK radius 1 voxel), `min_component_fraction` replace `closing_kernel`, `opening_kernel`, `keep_largest`; `bone_subtraction_dilation_radius` removed (bones always win over muscles).

### Removed
- `tools/run_steps.py` (use `mskpipe run`).

### Fixed (vs. BP pipeline)
- BP `VotingBinaryIterativeHoleFilling` grew masks instead of filling cavities; BP binarization was correct only for uint8 masks; BP cleaned masks could overlap (e.g. femur/hip).
- MuscleMap exits with code 0 on errors and stores labels with the input header (8-bit inputs scale label IDs lossily): the output is checked and 8-bit inputs get a float32 copy.
